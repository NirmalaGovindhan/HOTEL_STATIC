Yercaud is a serene hill station nestled in the Shevaroy Hills of the Eastern Ghats in Tamil Nadu, India. it is often referred to as the "Jewel of the South" due to its captivating natural beauty and tranquil ambiance. Unlike more commercialized hill stations, Yercaud retains a quaint, pristine charm characterized by vast coffee plantations, aromatic spice gardens, and dense orange groves. The heart of the town features the scenic emerald Yercaud Lake, surrounded by lush greenery and gardens. With its refreshing, year-round cool climate, mist-covered peaks, and panoramic viewpoints like Pagoda Point and Lady's Seat, Yercaud serves as a perfect, peaceful retreat for nature lovers and trekkers alike.
